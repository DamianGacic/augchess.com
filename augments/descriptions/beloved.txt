A handicap that pays you: drafting Beloved gives you 2 extra points to spend instead of costing any. In return, your Queen becomes a second King. She can never be left under attack: she may not step where the enemy could take her, a piece shielding her may not step aside, and when she is attacked you must deal with it, just like check. If no move gets both your King and your Queen out of danger, it's checkmate. Losing her in any way (a capture, an arrow, a spell, an aura) loses the game on the spot, so killing the enemy's Beloved Queen wins at once, even while your own King is in check. Standing in an enemy Ring of Fire counts as check, and your own Solar Strike can never be aimed over her. Only the Queen you start with is Beloved; a promoted Queen is an ordinary piece. A heart marks her on the board.
