Exchanges your king for a Warlord. The Warlord moves just like a king, and his death or capture still ends the game — but he survives the first attempt to kill him, whatever it is: a capture, an arrow, a spell, an aura. If that first blow comes from a melee unit (anything that strikes by moving onto his square, a Ghoul's charge or a Troll's club), the attacker dies instead and the Warlord keeps his square. Until he has been hurt, the Warlord can never be put in check, so he may stand where enemies can reach him. Once hurt, a half-empty health bar hangs over his head: from then on he is a regular king, with check and checkmate as usual.
