Before the very first turn of the game, you get up to 30 free seconds (not taken from your clock) to move any of your units forward onto your Vanguard squares — for White d3, e3, c4, d4, e4, f4, d5 and e5; for Black d6, e6, c5, d5, e5, f5, d4 and e4. Fill as many as you like, or press Deploy to finish early; when time runs out, whatever you planned deploys. Your opponent only sees the result, when all units march out at once. If you play White, your Vanguard units can't act on your first turn. After a deployment, Breaking Rank and Warm Welcome (for both players) stay inactive until your opponent has had their first turn.
