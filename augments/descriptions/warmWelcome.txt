Requires Archers. A passive trap — no casting, just an ever-ready slot on your global row. Any enemy unit that moves onto a square one of your Archers threatens is shot down for free at the end of that same turn, without spending a turn of your own. Afterward the trap needs 4 of your own turns to reset — and any of your Archers firing a normal shot also resets that cooldown, so the trap and manual shooting draw on the same nerve.
