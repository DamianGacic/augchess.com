Your bishops carry shields. A shield covers one side of its bishop, straight or diagonal, and blocks every attack from that side: enemies can't capture the bishop from there, and arrows, fireballs and bullets fired from there bounce off harmlessly. Shields start out facing forward. Whenever a bishop moves, turning its shield is the last step of your turn: click one of the squares next to it to face the shield that way, or click the bishop to keep it as it is. Attacks with no clear direction (a knight's jump, Solar Strike, auras) ignore shields. Can't be combined with Longbowmen.
