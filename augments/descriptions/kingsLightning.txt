A King ability, usable once per game. Select your King and press W (or click its slot) to call down a permanent aura of lightning around him — every square touching him, in every direction. Casting Lightning Aura uses up your turn — you do not also get to make a normal move. Once active it never turns off: from then on, any unit standing on one of those squares at the end of either player's turn is executed, friend or foe, and it keeps re-checking every square your King moves next to for the rest of the game.
