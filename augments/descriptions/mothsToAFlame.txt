Your charming Queen's pull becomes deadly. An enemy unit already standing next to her no longer stays put: if it is the closest one in her line of sight, it steps onto her square at the end of your turn and dies to her. This works on the King too, so ending your turn with your Queen next to the enemy King wins the game, unless he is a fresh Warlord, who survives it once, wounded. If several enemies are equally close, one of them is chosen at random.
