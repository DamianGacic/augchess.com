A global ability, usable once per game. Press its slot on your global ability row (A/S/D/F) to commit your army to a relentless advance. Casting it uses up your turn — you do not also get to make a normal move. Once active it never turns off: at the end of every one of your turns from then on, 2 of your pawn-type units are chosen at random and each takes a step forward into the free square ahead of it. If a chosen unit has no free square ahead but an enemy stands there or on either square diagonally ahead, it strikes that unit down instead. A unit boxed in on all three squares simply stays put that turn.
