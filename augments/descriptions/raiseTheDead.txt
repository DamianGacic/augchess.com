Requires Ghoul Corps. A King ability — select your King and press R to begin. Valid squares are any free square touching your King, plus, in each of the 8 files, the single free square nearest your own back rank with nothing free standing behind it (an ally, an enemy, or the back rank's edge all count as "blocked" — only another free square behind it disqualifies it). Click any number of these squares, one at a time, to raise a fallen Ghoul on each — you're limited by how many of your Ghouls have actually died so far this game. Casting doesn't end your turn by itself: keep raising as many as you have and want, then either run out (it ends automatically) or press Z to end your turn early and keep whatever you've raised. Once used, it can't be used again on your very next turn.
