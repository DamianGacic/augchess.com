A handicap that pays you: drafting Patchy Lines gives you 1 extra point to spend instead of costing any. In return, your army marches out 3 pawns short — which ones is chosen at random. Before the very first turn of the game, you get up to 10 free seconds (not taken from your clock) to slide your remaining pawns side to side along their row, so you decide where the gaps end up. Press Ready to finish early; when time runs out, whatever you arranged stands. Your opponent only sees the result. If you exchange your pawns for another unit, such as Ghouls or Archers, those units are the ones missing; with Legion, the 3 gaps can fall in either row, and each unit slides only along its own row. With Vanguard, the arrangement comes first and the deployment follows.
