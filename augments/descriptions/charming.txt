Your Queen draws enemies to her. At the end of each of your turns, the closest enemy unit that can see her along a straight or diagonal line takes one step towards her along that line, whether it could normally move that way or not. Line of sight is blocked by any unit in between, friend or foe. A unit already standing next to her stays where it is, and also blocks anything behind it. If several enemies are equally close, one of them is chosen at random. Manned towers are immune and block her line of sight. Any unit can be drawn in, the King included, and a pawn drawn onto its promotion row becomes a Queen. Only the Queen you start with is charming; a promoted Queen is an ordinary piece. A light pink ring marks where the charmed unit landed.
