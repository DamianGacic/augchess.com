Chess, augmented

Break the rules.

Classic chess, until someone bids on an augment. Before every match, both players spend points on new units, abilities and rule-breaking powers. Draft carefully: a bad draft can lose the game before the first move.

How a match works

Three phases, one winner.

01

Set the stakes

Agree on a time control and how many auction points each side gets to spend.

02

Draft augments

Take turns picking augments. Swap your pawns for Archers or Ghouls, arm your King with Silver Bullet, or unlock follow-up upgrades.

03

Play it out

Everything else is ordinary chess. Use abilities with Q/W/E/R for the selected unit and A/S/D/F for global powers.
